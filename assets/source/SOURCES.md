# NEAL photographic source register

The user-supplied source below is cleared for this project based on the user's rights attestation. Other sources remain blocked unless their commercial and derivative-editing rights are documented.

## Required evidence

For every source photo, record:

- Original filename and unmodified original file
- Photographer or copyright owner
- Canonical source URL
- License name and license URL, or a copy of written permission
- Confirmation that commercial use is allowed
- Confirmation that cropping, background removal, compositing, colour correction, and other derivative edits are allowed
- Required credit line
- Date obtained
- SHA-256 checksum of the original

## Rejected shortcuts

- News-site copies and screenshots: publication credit is not a reusable license.
- AP/news thumbnails: previews are not licensed token-art sources.
- Generic southern elephant-seal stock: rights may be available, but it would not be a real photo of Neil.
- AI-generated photorealism: it would not satisfy the real-photo direction.
